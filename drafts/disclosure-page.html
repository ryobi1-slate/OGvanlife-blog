Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on OG Vanlife are affiliate links. If you buy through them, OG Vanlife earns a commission at no cost to you. That is the business model. Here is how it is kept from corrupting the engineering.

The rules

  1. Parts are chosen on the engineering first: specs, load math, manufacturer documentation.
  2. Affiliate availability is checked second.
  3. If the right part has no affiliate program, it gets recommended anyway.
  4. A part is never swapped for a different one to earn a commission.

The incentive, stated plainly

A commission rewards recommending parts that are easy to sell and expensive. Treat every recommendation the way you would treat a contractor's: check the math yourself. Calculations are shown on the page so you can.
